OUR PROCESS

From Idea to Reality

01 — Understanding

We begin by understanding your requirements, lifestyle, business, preferences, budget and vision.

02 — Concept

We develop the overall design direction through layouts, mood boards, materials, colours and visual references.

03 — Visualisation

Our 3D visualisations allow you to experience the proposed space before execution begins.

04 — Planning

Detailed drawings, material specifications and execution planning are developed to translate the design into reality.

05 — Execution

Our team coordinates the required trades, materials and site work while maintaining the design intent.

06 — Finishing

The final stage focuses on detailing, furniture, lighting, décor and finishing touches that bring the entire space together.

07 — Handover

Once the work is completed, we ensure that the finished space reflects the approved design and meets the required standards.
